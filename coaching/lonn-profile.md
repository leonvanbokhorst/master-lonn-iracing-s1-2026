## Season Observations

### Week 01 – Summit Point Jefferson

**Key Breakthrough:** Time comes from transitions, not corner speed.

**The Numbers:**
| Metric | Start | End | Δ |
|--------|-------|-----|---|
| Best lap | 51.438s | 50.985s | −0.453s |
| Consistency (σ) | 5.22s | 0.26s | −4.96s |
| Coasting % | 12.2% | 1.7% | −10.5% |
| Gap to optimal | 2.8s | 0.19s | −2.6s |

**Pattern Discovered:** Coasting elimination was the big unlock. Not braking later, not more corner speed – just filling the dead time between brake and throttle.

**Laps to Settle Trend:** 5 → 2 → 1 → 12 (race)

- By Event #3, found rhythm in ONE lap
- Race reset to 12 laps (expected – new variables)

**Event #2 Anomaly:** Coasting went UP (12% → 14.5%) but pace improved. Hypothesis: was locking in the _line_ before optimizing _transitions_. Worth asking about.

**Race Insight:** σ of 0.26s in traffic = tightest of the week. Performs well under pressure. This is important.

**Incidents:** 14x in AI race – mix of racing and "lazy off-tracks." Needs honesty here, not excuses.

**Carry Forward Question:** Does the coasting fix transfer to Rudskogen, or was it Jefferson-specific?
