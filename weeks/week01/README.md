### Key Learnings

1. **Coasting elimination** was the biggest technique improvement (12% → 2%)
2. **Lap family** formed by Event #3 – consistent siblings, not random strangers
3. **Race rhythm survived** – σ of 0.26s in traffic proves the skill transferred
4. **Gap to optimal** closed from 2.8s to under 0.2s

## Reflection

- **Track craft:** Jefferson rewards smooth transitions. The time comes from filling the gaps between brake and throttle, not from braking later.
- **Brake bias:** 55.0% worked well. 54% was too pointy.
- **Mindset:** "Don't chase the time, let it come" worked. Best lap came when not trying.

### Next Week Intent

- Carry this rhythm to a new track
- Focus on "laps to settle" – can we find pace faster?
- Maintain clean incident count in official races
